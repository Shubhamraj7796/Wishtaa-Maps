The short version

  • You can use Wishtaa Maps without an account. Then your files stay in your browser on your device, and we receive nothing about them.
  • If you create an account, we keep only your sign-in details, display name, a few preferences, a list of recently opened files (names and dates) and which Google account you linked for Drive.
  • Your maps and pictures are not stored on our servers. They stay in your browser, or in your own Google Drive if you choose that.

What we store when you have an account

  • Sign-in: your email address, how you sign in (emailed code, password, Google) and, for a password, a salted hash (we never see your password). If you use Google, your Google name, email address and profile picture link.
  • Profile: your display name.
  • Preferences: a few choices such as light or dark theme.
  • Recent files: for each recently opened file, its name, the date, whether it is in this browser or in Google Drive, and for Drive files the Drive file id. Never the content. You can switch off listing files saved in your browser on the Account page.
  • Drive link: the Google address you linked for Drive and whether it is connected. Never an access token.

What we do not store

  • The content of your maps, notes, PDFs or pictures.
  • Your Google password, or any Google access token on our servers. The short-lived Drive access token is kept only in your browser's memory and is gone when you close the tab.

Google Drive and the permission we ask for

If you connect Google Drive we ask for one permission, drive.file. It lets Wishtaa Maps create and open only the files it creates, or that you pick with it. It cannot see or list the rest of your Drive. Signing in with Google additionally shares your name, email address and profile picture, to identify you.

Wishtaa Maps's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to provide the features you see, we do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it except with your permission, to fix a problem you report, or where the law requires.

You can remove the access at any time from the Account page ("Disconnect Google Drive") or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Information kept in your browser

The app saves your files, settings and sign-in session in your browser's storage (local storage, IndexedDB and a service-worker cache) so it works offline. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Anyone who can use your browser profile can see files saved there, so use separate browser profiles on a shared computer. Signing out removes your Drive access and the cached Drive list from the browser; files saved as "This browser" stay.
